# DevOps & CI/CD Architectural Logo

Create a premium logo for **DEVOPS & CI/CD PIPELINES** using the same natural architectural
identity as the poster.

Create a compact architectural emblem consisting of two interconnected contemporary
concrete-and-glass buildings with:
- planted rooftops
- terraces
- trees
- balconies
- bridges
- natural landscaping

The buildings should subtly form a continuous loop representing CI/CD without using the
traditional DevOps infinity symbol.

Use a warm ivory background and realistic but restrained architectural materials.

Wordmark:
DEVOPS

Subtitle:
& CI/CD PIPELINES

Use Bricolage Grotesque-inspired bold geometric typography for DEVOPS and Figtree for the
subtitle. Integrate a small muted-sage leaf subtly into the O.

Palette:
charcoal #252B26
muted sage #A3A58A
natural green
warm concrete grey
natural wood
warm ivory #F4F1EA

The result must work as a technology brand, conference identity, favicon basis and editorial
publication mark.

Avoid futuristic architecture, neon, circuitry, holograms, cyberpunk, cartoon graphics,
generic technology symbols and excessive detail.
