# Image Prompt Collection

## Poster

Create a premium vertical editorial poster titled “DEVOPS & CI/CD PIPELINES” showing a realistic
sustainable technology colony under natural daylight. Six contemporary concrete-and-glass
districts represent CODE, BUILD, TEST, PACKAGE, DEPLOY and MONITOR, connected physically through
roads, bridges and utility infrastructure. Use lush rooftop gardens, trees, warm concrete,
glass reflections, realistic weathering, blue sky, soft clouds and architectural photography.
Typography uses Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree and JetBrains Mono. Avoid futuristic architecture,
neon, holograms, cyberpunk and generic SaaS imagery.

## Logo

Create a minimal architectural logo for “DEVOPS & CI/CD PIPELINES” using two interconnected
sustainable concrete-and-glass buildings with rooftop gardens, terraces, trees and a bridge.
Subtly communicate continuous delivery without using the conventional DevOps infinity symbol.
Use warm ivory, charcoal, muted sage, natural green and concrete grey. Integrate a small leaf
into the O of DEVOPS.

## WebGL City Reference

Create a realistic contemporary sustainable technology colony from an elevated architectural
photography perspective. Use concrete buildings, glass, wood, rooftop gardens, mature trees,
roads, bridges, pedestrians, vehicles and distant city atmosphere. Organise the colony into six
physical districts: CODE, BUILD, TEST, PACKAGE, DEPLOY and MONITOR. Natural late-afternoon light,
realistic shadows and atmospheric perspective. No futuristic architecture, neon or holograms.
